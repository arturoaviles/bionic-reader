# Bionic Reader

A text-reading tool that presents existing text with emphasis on word beginnings.

## Language

**Bionic reading**:
A reading style that emphasizes the beginning of selected words while preserving their text and layout.
_Avoid_: Text rewriting, speed-reading guarantee

**Emphasis length**:
The portion of a selected word's beginning that receives emphasis.
_Avoid_: Boldness

**Emphasis frequency**:
How often words in a passage are selected to receive emphasis.
_Avoid_: Emphasis length

**Reading session**:
An opt-in interactive shell session in which command output is presented in bionic reading style.
_Avoid_: Global text rewriting

**Passthrough**:
Presentation of output without adding bionic reading emphasis, preserving the content and existing formatting.
_Avoid_: Formatting removal
